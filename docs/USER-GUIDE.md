# ITAM Console: User guide

For everyday users of the application. No technical knowledge needed.

## 1. Signing in
1. Open the application link your administrator gave you.
2. Click **Continue with company sign-in**.
3. First time only: enter your email and the **temporary password** you received by email, then choose a new password (at least 12 characters, with a symbol).
4. Next time, sign in with your email and new password. Use the sign-out button at the bottom of the left menu when finished.

## 2. Your role decides what you can do

| Role | What you can do |
|---|---|
| Viewer | Look at assets, tickets and licenses; raise a ticket |
| Technician | Everything a viewer can, plus create and edit assets, update tickets, upload files |
| Admin | Everything, plus delete, manage licenses, and read the audit log |

If a button is missing, your role does not allow that action. Ask an administrator to change your role.

## 3. Dashboard
Shows total assets, how many are assigned, in stock or in repair, the ticket queue, the latest tickets, and licenses about to expire.

## 4. Assets
- **Find an asset:** use the search box, or the filters (All, In stock, Assigned, In repair, Retired).
- **Add an asset** (technician or admin): click **Add asset**, fill in the name, serial number, category and status. Assigned-to, location and warranty date are optional. Click **Save**. The system gives it a tag such as `AST-000009`.
- **Edit or delete:** open the asset. Edit is available to technicians; delete to admins only.
- **Attach a file:** open the asset, click **Upload file**, choose a PDF or image up to 10 MB (invoice, warranty, photo).

## 5. Tickets
- Click **Raise ticket**, choose the asset, describe the issue and pick a priority (low, medium, high, critical).
- Technicians change the status: open, in progress, resolved, closed.

## 6. Licenses (admin)
Add the product, vendor, seats you own and seats used, yearly cost and expiry date. The system emails an alert 30, 14 and 7 days before expiry.

## 7. Audit log (admin)
Lists every change with who made it, when, and what changed.

## 8. Common questions

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| I forgot my password | Use the **Forgot your password?** link on the sign-in page |
| I see "Sign-in session expired" | Close the tab and open the application link again |
| An upload fails | Check the file is under 10 MB; ask an administrator if it keeps failing |
| I cannot delete an asset | Only administrators can delete |
